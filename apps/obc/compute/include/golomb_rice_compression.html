Explore how a number is encoded using Golomb-Rice coding.

Step 1: Determine Divisor (M)
In Rice coding, the divisor M is always a power of 2.
M = 22 = 4
Step 2: Calculate Quotient (q) and Remainder (r)
Quotient (q) = N ÷ M = 13 ÷ 4 = 3
Remainder (r) = N modulo M = 13 % 4 = 1
Step 3: Encode
Encode quotient 3 in Unary: 1110
Encode remainder 1 in Binary (using exactly 2 bits): 01

Final Encoded Bitstream:

111001
